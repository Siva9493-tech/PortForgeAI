How it works

How PortForge AI Works

From raw information to a shareable portfolio link — a guided five-step workflow with no blank page in sight.

  1. 01Step 1

    Import Your Information

    Connect GitHub, upload your resume, or import LinkedIn to quickly bring your professional information into PortForge AI.

  2. 02Step 2

    AI Understands Your Profile

    Our AI analyzes your experience, projects, skills, and achievements to organize everything intelligently.

  3. 03Step 3

    Choose Your Portfolio Style

    Select a professional portfolio theme that matches your personality and career goals.

  4. 04Step 4

    Generate & Customize

    Instantly generate your portfolio and customize every section before publishing.

  5. 05Step 5

    Publish & Share

    Deploy your portfolio and share your personalized portfolio link with recruiters and companies.
